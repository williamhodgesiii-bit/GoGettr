The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

You do not need more motivation. You need fewer decisions.

• Same wake window, every day.
• Automatic transfers on payday.
• Training clothes staged before bed.
• Tomorrow planned before today ends.
• Remove the daily negotiation.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
