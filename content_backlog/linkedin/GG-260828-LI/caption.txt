The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

Your phone is either a tool or a slot machine. Which did you build?

• Move feeds off the first screen.
• Put calendar and tasks where your thumb lands.
• Turn off alerts that create no action.
• Keep banking, notes and maps one tap away.
• Your home screen is an environment.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
