The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

Before the next yes, buy, quit or delay — run this test.

• Will tomorrow-you thank you?
• Will one-year-you still care?
• Will five-year-you pay for it?
• If the answer worsens with time, change the move.
• Decide for the person who inherits it.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
