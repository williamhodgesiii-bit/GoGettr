The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

Checkout can wait 60 seconds. Run the first check.

• Will you use it 50 times?
• What is the cost per use?
• Are you buying the logo or the function?
• Would you still buy it without financing?
• If the answers are weak, leave the tab open.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
