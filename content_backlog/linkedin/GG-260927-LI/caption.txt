The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

Three days, no delivery apps. Notice what the convenience was solving.

• Delete the apps for 72 hours.
• Cook or walk to it.
• Notice the real cost you were paying.
• Keep the money you did not spend.
• Decide what stays deleted.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
