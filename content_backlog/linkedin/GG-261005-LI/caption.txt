The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

Mail has two jobs: act or archive. Give both a place.

• One tray to ACT.
• One tray to FILE.
• Nothing lands anywhere else.
• Clear the ACT tray weekly.
• Stop moving paper around the house.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
