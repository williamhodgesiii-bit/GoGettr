The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

One account for bills. One for spending. Never mix them.

• Bills and savings live in account one.
• Spending money lives in account two.
• Only account two has a card on you.
• When it is empty, you are done.
• Let the structure hold the discipline.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
