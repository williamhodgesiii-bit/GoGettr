The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

The bill was not a surprise. You just did not name it.

• List the once-a-year costs.
• Divide each by twelve.
• Set aside that slice monthly.
• Let the fund fill before the bill.
• A named cost stops ambushing you.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
