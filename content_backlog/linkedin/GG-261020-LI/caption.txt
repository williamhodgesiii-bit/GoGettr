The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

A task with no box expands to eat your whole day.

• Estimate the task honestly.
• Give it a fixed box on the calendar.
• Start and stop with the box.
• Unfinished rolls to the next box.
• The box protects the rest of the day.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
