The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

Your to-do list is full because your not-to-do list is empty.

• List what you always regret doing.
• No meetings without an agenda.
• No phone in the first hour.
• No yes on the day of the ask.
• What you refuse protects what you finish.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
