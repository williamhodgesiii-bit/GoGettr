The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

Stop asking how am I doing. Ask what they can answer.

• Ask what one thing to change.
• Ask where you slowed the team.
• Ask what a raise would require.
• Say thanks, then actually do it.
• Specific questions unlock real answers.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
