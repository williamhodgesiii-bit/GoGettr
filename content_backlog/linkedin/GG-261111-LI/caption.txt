The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

Want a yes on a favor? Ask for advice instead.

• Trade the big ask for a small one.
• Ask how they would approach it.
• Let them invest a little first.
• Report back what you tried.
• Advice opens a door a favor closes.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
