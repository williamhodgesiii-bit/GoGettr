The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

Pair the thing you should do with the thing you want to.

• Favorite show only on the treadmill.
• The good coffee only while you plan.
• Bundle the want to the should.
• Let one carry the other.
• Make the right move the fun one.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
