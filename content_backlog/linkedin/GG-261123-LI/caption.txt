The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

Your inbox is a to-do list other people keep writing.

• Do, delegate, defer, or delete.
• Touch each message one time.
• Unsubscribe instead of deleting again.
• Archive the moment it is handled.
• Decide once, not ten times.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
