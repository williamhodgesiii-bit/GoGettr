The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

Wake to a made scene, not to last night's mess.

• Clear one main surface fully.
• Start the dishwasher, not the pile.
• Reset the room to zero.
• Lay out tomorrow's first move.
• Future-you inherits the calm.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
