The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

Do not trust future-you. Remove the option to quit.

• Pay in advance, non-refundable.
• Tell someone who will check.
• Put a stake on the outcome.
• Make quitting cost something.
• Bind future-you to today's plan.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
