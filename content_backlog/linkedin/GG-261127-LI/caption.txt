The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

Multitasking is just switching costs you cannot see.

• One tab, one task, one timer.
• Close what you are not using.
• Park stray thoughts on a pad.
• Finish before you start the next.
• Focus is a subtraction game.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
