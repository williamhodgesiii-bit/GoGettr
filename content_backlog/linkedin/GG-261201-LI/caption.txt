The most capable people I know don't run on willpower. They design defaults.

For five days, walk ten minutes after every meal.

• Ten minutes, right after eating.
• No pace goal, just move.
• Notice the steadier energy after.
• Notice the easier evenings.
• Let five days make the case.

Systems beat motivation because they only ask you to decide once.
